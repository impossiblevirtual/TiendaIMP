Try a week of content ideas — free

A simple starter plan for small businesses that want to post more consistently without guessing what to say.

Adapt the examples to your niche, brand voice and real offers. You do not have to post every day: choose a sustainable rhythm.

Monday — Introduce your business

Idea: Show what you do, who you help and what makes your service useful.

Caption prompt: “New here? We’re [business name], helping [audience] with [service]. What would you like to know about us?”

Call to action: Follow us or send us a message with your question.

Tuesday — Helpful tip

Idea: Share one practical tip your customer can use today.

Caption prompt: “Quick tip: [useful tip]. Save this post so you can come back to it.”

Call to action: Save this tip or share it with someone who needs it.

Wednesday — Highlight a service

Idea: Explain one service or product and the problem it solves.

Caption prompt: “If you need [result], our [service/product] may help because [benefit].”

Call to action: Message us to ask whether it fits your needs.

Thursday — FAQ

Idea: Answer a question customers often ask before buying.

Caption prompt: “One question we hear often is: [question]. Here’s the answer: [answer].”

Call to action: Leave your next question in the comments.

Friday — Behind the scenes

Idea: Show a process, workspace, preparation or the people behind the business.

Caption prompt: “A little look behind the scenes at how we prepare [product/service].”

Call to action: What would you like to see next?

Saturday — Customer needs

Idea: Describe a common problem and a helpful way to address it, without inventing testimonials.

Caption prompt: “Struggling with [problem]? Start by [practical first step].”

Call to action: Send us a message if you want to explore your options.

Sunday — Weekly recap

Idea: Summarize the week's tips and preview next week's topic.

Caption prompt: “This week we covered [topic 1], [topic 2] and [topic 3]. Next week: [topic].”

Call to action: Follow along and tell us what topic you want next.
