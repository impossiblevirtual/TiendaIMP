How to set your freelance rate

A sustainable rate should account for your target income, business expenses, the hours you can realistically bill, and a buffer for time off and unexpected costs.

1. Calculate your baseline

Add your desired monthly income and business expenses. Revenue is not the same as take-home income; set aside the amounts required for taxes and other obligations in your country.

2. Estimate billable hours realistically

Admin, prospecting, proposals and learning take time too. Do not assume every working hour can be billed to a client.

3. Define project scope

Estimate working hours, meetings, revisions and deliverables. Write down what is included and how out-of-scope changes will be handled.

4. Check your market

Compare similar services, experience, language, complexity and evidence of your skills. There is no universal rate that fits every freelancer.
