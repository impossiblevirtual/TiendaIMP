Your checklist for starting a freelance project clearly

A practical checklist to prevent misunderstandings before you start working with a client. Save it and reuse it for every new project.

Before accepting the work

  • Confirm the client's goal and the problem they need solved.
  • Define deliverables and what is outside the scope.
  • Agree on revisions, deadlines and delivery format.
  • Set the price, currency, payment method and any deposit.
  • Choose one main channel for messages and approvals.

When the project starts

  • Collect the required materials, access and references.
  • Send a written summary of the agreed scope.
  • Share milestones and the next review date.
  • Document scope changes before doing extra work.
  • Get written approval for important deliverables.

Before closing the project

  • Test files and links before sending them.
  • Explain what was delivered and how to use it.
  • Remind the client of any outstanding balance.
  • Ask for feedback and permission before using the work in your portfolio.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a contract?

For substantial projects, consider a written agreement covering scope, payment, deadlines, revisions and cancellations. Legal requirements vary by country.

Should I work without a deposit?

That depends on the project and your agreement. Put the terms in writing before work begins to reduce confusion.

Can I use this checklist with every client?

Use it as a starting point and adapt it to the service, budget and project complexity.
