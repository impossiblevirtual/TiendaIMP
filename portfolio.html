A sample social media calendar for a fictional independent clothing boutique — designed to balance product discovery, useful advice, community engagement and sales prompts.

Portfolio disclaimer: This is a fictional concept created to demonstrate our planning approach. The boutique, content and campaign are illustrative; this is not client work and no performance results are claimed.

The approach

A practical mix, not 30 sales pitches

The calendar alternates product stories, styling ideas, behind-the-scenes content, audience questions and clear calls to action. Adapt the wording, images, inventory, prices and promotions to the real business before publishing.

  • Audience: people shopping for everyday clothing and outfit inspiration.
  • Primary goal: encourage relevant conversations and product enquiries.
  • Suggested formats: Reels, carousels, single-image posts and Stories.
  • Voice: friendly, confident and helpful.

30-day sample calendar

Example plan for a clothing boutique

Day 1 · Brand introduction

“Welcome in! We believe getting dressed should feel like you. Tell us: what three words describe your style?”

Format: Reel or carousel · CTA: Comment below

Day 2 · Best-seller spotlight

“Meet one of our everyday favourites. How would you style it: casual, smart or somewhere in between?”

Format: Product photo · CTA: Share your pick

Day 3 · Styling tip

“One piece, three looks: build a relaxed outfit, a work-ready look and an evening option around the same staple.”

Format: Reel · CTA: Save for later

Day 4 · Behind the scenes

“A little look at how we prepare new arrivals. Which detail do you notice first: colour, fabric or fit?”

Format: Stories · CTA: Vote in a poll

Day 5 · Fit and fabric

“Shopping online is easier when the details are clear. We’ll show the texture, fit and movement so you can choose with confidence.”

Format: Close-up carousel · CTA: Ask a question

Day 6 · Community question

“What is the one item you reach for again and again? We’re collecting ideas for future styling posts.”

Format: Stories · CTA: Reply

Day 7 · Weekly recap

“Missed a post this week? Here are three outfit ideas worth saving. Which one should we expand into a full look?”

Format: Carousel · CTA: Choose 1, 2 or 3

Day 8 · New arrival

“New in: a fresh option to add to your rotation. Check the available sizes and colours before choosing your favourite.”

Format: Reel · CTA: Message for availability

Day 9 · Outfit formula

“Try this simple formula: one base colour, one contrasting layer and one accessory that brings it together.”

Format: Carousel · CTA: Save this idea

Day 10 · FAQ

“Wondering about sizing? Send us the item you’re considering and we’ll help you check the available measurements.”

Format: Stories · CTA: Send a message

Day 11 · Colour pairing

“Which palette feels more like you: neutrals, earth tones or a pop of colour? Pick your favourite combination.”

Format: Carousel · CTA: Comment your choice

Day 12 · Product details

“The small details matter. Take a closer look at the finish, seams and fabric of this piece.”

Format: Close-up video · CTA: Ask about the item

Day 13 · Style challenge

“Your challenge: create two different looks using the same basic item. Which version would you wear first?”

Format: Reel · CTA: Vote

Day 14 · Customer care

“Not sure what to choose? Tell us the occasion and the style you like; we’ll point you toward relevant options.”

Format: Single image or Stories · CTA: Send a message

Day 15 · Mid-month edit

“A mid-month edit of versatile pieces that can work across more than one occasion. Which would you wear most?”

Format: Carousel · CTA: Comment

Day 16 · How to care

“Help your favourite pieces last longer: always check the garment care label and follow its washing instructions.”

Format: Carousel · CTA: Save the tip

Day 17 · Styling detail

“A small styling change can shift the whole outfit. Compare the look with and without a layer or accessory.”

Format: Before/after Reel · CTA: Which look wins?

Day 18 · Stock update

“Looking for a particular size or colour? Message us to confirm current availability before placing an order.”

Format: Stories · CTA: Enquire

Day 19 · Myth or tip

“Style tip: you don’t need a completely new wardrobe to try a new look. Start by recombining pieces you already own.”

Format: Talking Reel · CTA: Share with a friend

Day 20 · This or that

“Choose your weekend mood: relaxed layers or a more polished outfit? We want to know what to feature next.”

Format: Stories poll · CTA: Vote

Day 21 · Three ways

“Three ways to style one staple: keep it simple, add a layer or build the look around a statement accessory.”

Format: Carousel · CTA: Save for inspiration

Day 22 · Product pairing

“Build a coordinated outfit by starting with one piece you love, then choosing colours and shapes that complement it.”

Format: Flat lay · CTA: Ask for suggestions

Day 23 · Behind the brand

“What should we show more often: new arrivals, styling advice, fabric details or outfit ideas? Help shape our next posts.”

Format: Stories · CTA: Vote

Day 24 · Gift inspiration

“Shopping for someone else? Start with their everyday style and check sizing or exchange details before choosing.”

Format: Carousel · CTA: Ask us for help

Day 25 · Frequently asked question

“A quick reminder: availability can change. Contact us for the latest details on sizes, colours and delivery options.”

Format: Single image · CTA: Message us

Day 26 · Outfit remix

“Revisit a favourite piece and style it for a different occasion. The goal is more combinations from what you already own.”

Format: Reel · CTA: Save this idea

Day 27 · Community feature

“We love seeing how people make a look their own. Share your outfit with us; ask permission before reposting customer content.”

Format: Stories · CTA: Tag or message

Day 28 · Product selection

“Choosing between two options? Compare the fit, fabric and how each piece works with items you already wear.”

Format: Comparison carousel · CTA: Tell us your pick

Day 29 · Gentle sales prompt

“Found something you like? Send us the product name or screenshot and we’ll confirm current availability and price.”

Format: Product post · CTA: Enquire

Day 30 · Month-end recap

“That’s a month of outfit ideas! Which topic should return next month: styling, new arrivals, fit advice or behind the scenes?”

Format: Reel or carousel · CTA: Vote and follow
