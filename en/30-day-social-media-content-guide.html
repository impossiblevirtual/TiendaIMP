Free guide: 30 social media content ideas for your business

A month of posts doesn't have to start from scratch every week. Use these prompts as a starting point, adapt them to your industry, and choose ideas that help your customers.

How to use this guide

Choose two to four ideas per week based on your capacity. Mix helpful content, product highlights, trust-building and calls to action. Use your own photos when possible, and never invent testimonials or results.

30 ideas to plan your month

  1. 1. Introduce your business and explain who you help.
  2. 2. Feature your most requested product or service.
  3. 3. Answer a frequently asked customer question.
  4. 4. Share a useful tip related to your industry.
  5. 5. Explain how to use or book your service.
  6. 6. Show a behind-the-scenes work process.
  7. 7. Introduce the people behind the business.
  8. 8. Compare two options and explain who each suits.
  9. 9. Debunk a common myth in your field.
  10. 10. Share a short list of practical recommendations.
  11. 11. Explain the problem your product helps solve.
  12. 12. Show what makes your offer different.
  13. 13. Ask a question to learn customer preferences.
  14. 14. Share a genuine review with the customer's permission.
  15. 15. Explain how to choose between products or plans.
  16. 16. Show a real before-and-after with context.
  17. 17. Address a common objection without pressuring people.
  18. 18. Explain the steps to order or book.
  19. 19. Feature a new or seasonal offer.
  20. 20. Share a common mistake and how to avoid it.
  21. 21. Show how to care for or maintain what you sell.
  22. 22. Create a short step-by-step guide.
  23. 23. Tell a brief story about how your business started.
  24. 24. Invite people to save a useful post.
  25. 25. Run a poll between two relevant options.
  26. 26. Share accurate availability or important dates.
  27. 27. Explain clearly what your price includes.
  28. 28. Answer a question received by message without exposing private details.
  29. 29. Remind people how to contact or buy from you.
  30. 30. Recap the month's most useful tips and invite questions.

You don't need to publish every idea. Choose what matters to your audience, vary formats, and track which posts lead to real inquiries.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to post every day?

No. Treat these prompts as a content bank and choose a posting rhythm your business can maintain.

Do these ideas guarantee sales?

No. They help you plan communication, but results depend on your offer, audience, content quality and other factors.

Can I adapt them to any industry?

Yes. Adjust examples, language, photos, prices and calls to action to fit your actual business.
