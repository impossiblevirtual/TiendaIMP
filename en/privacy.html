Privacy

Last updated: October 14, 2026.

We want you to feel comfortable asking questions. This page explains, in plain language, what information Sepce Studio may receive when you use our contact form.

Information we receive

You may share your name, email address, business type, the service you are interested in, and any details you choose to include. A payment reference is optional.

How we use it

We use the information to reply to your enquiry, clarify the service scope and, if you choose to proceed, prepare or coordinate your content calendar. We do not publish your contact details or sell them to advertisers.

Third-party services

The contact form is processed through FormSubmit. Payments are handled by PayPal or the relevant service marketplace, such as Contra. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies. Do not enter passwords or full card details in the Sepce Studio contact form.

Access or deletion requests

To ask about information you submitted or request deletion of information we retain, email indumentariaimpossible@gmail.com.

A note

Please share only the information needed for your enquiry. This page describes our intended practices in plain language and is not a substitute for legal advice specific to the jurisdictions where the service is offered.
